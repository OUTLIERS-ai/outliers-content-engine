# My opinions: the quick-start list

> This is a filled copy of `your-voice/opinions/OPINIONS.md` for Sam Price, an invented bookkeeper
> for small trades businesses. Every opinion and every conversation is made up.

A brief names an opinion like this: `example/your-voice/opinions/OPINIONS.md entry 3`.

---

### 1. Sunday night books
> "If you're doing your own books on a Sunday night you've hired the most expensive bookkeeper in town, and it's you."

- **Where I said it:** to a builder on a discovery call, February
- **Why it would carry a post:** every tradesperson who does their own books thinks they're saving money. This says they're paying the most for the worst job.
- **Who would object:** owners who are proud of doing it all themselves
- **Tier:** 1

### 2. Busy right up to the end
> "Every builder I've watched go under was fully booked the month it happened."

- **Where I said it:** at a local business breakfast, March
- **Why it would carry a post:** it breaks the idea that a full diary means a safe business.
- **Who would object:** anyone who uses a full diary as proof they're doing fine
- **Tier:** 1

### 3. Quoting low
> "Quoting low to win the job is paying to go to work."

- **Where I said it:** to a decorator who'd lost money on 3 jobs in a row
- **Why it would carry a post:** short, and every trade has done it.
- **Who would object:** people who think low prices are how you get started
- **Tier:** 2

### 4. Invoice from the van
> "Send the invoice from the van before you drive off. Every day you leave it adds a week to getting paid."

- **Where I said it:** to an electrician chasing a pile of late invoices, May. His figures stay out of every post.
- **Why it would carry a post:** a habit anyone can change tomorrow, and a cost they've never counted.
- **Who would object:** people who say they're too tired after a job to do paperwork
- **Tier:** 1

### 5. The VAT account
> "The VAT was never your money. Move it to its own account the day it lands and stop looking at it."

- **Where I said it:** to a new client in March, the week before his VAT bill
- **Why it would carry a post:** most small firms in trouble with the tax office spent the VAT first.
- **Who would object:** owners who treat the VAT pot as a cash buffer
- **Tier:** 1

### 6. Worst-paying customers
> "If you can't name your 3 worst-paying customers, you're working for them."

- **Where I said it:** at a local business breakfast, March
- **Why it would carry a post:** a quick test the reader can run in their head, and most will fail it.
- **Who would object:** owners who think every customer is worth keeping
- **Tier:** 2

### 7. Once a year or every week
> "Your accountant sees your year once. I see it every week, and that's where the money gets saved."

- **Where I said it:** on a call with a plasterer who thought he only needed an accountant
- **Why it would carry a post:** names the difference between the 2 jobs in 2 sentences.
- **Who would object:** accountants, and owners who see bookkeeping as a cost
- **Tier:** 3

### 8. The van on finance
> "A new van on finance is the most common midlife crisis in the trades."

- **Where I said it:** laughing with a roofer, April
- **Why it would carry a post:** funny, true, and most of the audience has either done it or wants to.
- **Who would object:** anyone who has just bought one
- **Tier:** 2

### 9. January prices
> "Put your prices up every January and don't say sorry for it."

- **Where I said it:** to nearly every client, every December
- **Why it would carry a post:** a clear yearly habit with a date on it.
- **Who would object:** owners scared of losing customers
- **Tier:** 2

### 10. Friday's balance
> "Friday's bank balance is a liar. It doesn't know about Monday's direct debits."

- **Where I said it:** to a landscaper who'd bought a trailer on a good Friday
- **Why it would carry a post:** everyone has looked at a healthy balance and spent it.
- **Who would object:** owners who run the business off the bank app
- **Tier:** 3
