# EXAMPLE-WAVE: grounding

> This is a filled copy of `briefs/_TEMPLATE-WAVE/GROUNDING.md` for Sam Price, an invented
> bookkeeper for small trades businesses. Every figure is made up.

## The reader

- **What they earn or turn over:** a trades business with 1 to 5 people, turning over between
  150,000 and 600,000 pounds a year.
- **Their situation:** out on jobs from 7am, back home by 6, doing quotes and invoices at the
  kitchen table after the kids are in bed. A shoebox of receipts lives in the van.
- **What they believe about their problem:** paperwork is the price of being self-employed, and
  getting paid late is just how the trade works.
- **What they would call themselves:** "rubbish with numbers".

## Where the opinions come from

`example/your-voice/opinions/OPINIONS.md`, the 10 opinions Sam wrote by hand. This example wave uses
3 of them: entries 1, 4 and 5, across 2 subjects, doing your own books and getting paid. Sam's
first real wave uses all 10, 1 per post. Later waves use 20, once Sam has at least 20 opinions.

The quote lives in the opinion file and is the only material a writer may build on.

## What this wave is not about

- Tax advice on a specific case. Sam will not give it in public.
- Software. No post names an app or tells the reader what to buy.
- Sam's own service. No post mentions prices, packages or booking a call.

## Opinions skipped on purpose

None. This is Sam's first wave.

## What this wave is measured against

- **The stopping test, from START-HERE.md:** of the first 30 drafts this engine writes for Sam, at
  least 6 will be posted within 4 weeks of the review.
- **The baseline:** Sam's last 20 posts, all written by hand, averaged 3.1 comments per 1,000
  impressions. Sam's reach also dropped over the winter, so a better number from this engine could
  be reach recovering as much as better writing.
