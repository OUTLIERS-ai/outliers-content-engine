> **How to fill this in.** Open `my-posts.csv`, work out comments per 1,000 impressions for each
> post you wrote yourself (comments divided by impressions, times 1,000), and copy up to 9 of the
> highest here, best first. Paste each post exactly as it was published: typos, line breaks and all.
> Only posts you wrote. Never add a post a machine wrote, however well it did.
> Fill every field marked [FILL IN: ...]. Delete a slot you do not use.
> See `example/your-voice/THE-STANDARD.md` for a filled version.

> **For the writer. Read this before every wave.** These are my real published posts, ranked by
> comments per 1,000 impressions. Comments, not likes: a like is a reflex, a comment costs the
> reader effort. Do not extract rules from these posts. Do not summarise them. Read them, then
> write posts that could sit next to them without looking like a guest.
