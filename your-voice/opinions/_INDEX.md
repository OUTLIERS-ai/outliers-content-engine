> writes it in a separate run over the finished month files, following the index step at the end
> of `prompts/mine-a-month.md`. Each entry goes under 1 subject only: the subject a writer would
> look in first. Tier 1 first, then the rest by date.

> month file. Open the month file to get the verbatim quote, the transcript and the line number.

> a line the owner said.
>
> **How to read a line.** `T1 . 2026-03 #4` means entry 4 of `2026-03.md`, filed under Tier 1.
