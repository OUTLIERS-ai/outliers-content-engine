# Never mine: my exclusions

> **How to fill this in. Do this before any transcript is read.** Your calls contain other people's
> words, names, money and private lives. This file decides what the reader must never copy out,
> before the reading starts, so it is not left to judgement on the day.
> Step 1: read the standing categories below and add any of your own.
> Step 2: if you already know of a call or passage that must never be used, add a row to the table.
> Step 3: while mining, Claude adds a row every time it leaves a passage out, so the next reader
> knows it was seen and why it was skipped. The row says why. It never repeats the material.

## Standing categories: never mined, from any call

1. **Material that would be catastrophic if it surfaced.** Slurs, cruelty, any passage you would not want read out
   in court or to your family. Never quoted, never paraphrased, never hinted at.
2. **Other people's words.** A client's, a prospect's, a friend's. Only your own turns are mined.
3. **Other people's names and money.** No client or third party is named, and no figure about their
   business, income, debts or prices is used, even in your own words about them.
4. **Health, medical, family, partner and sexual material,** yours or anyone else's.
5. **Politics.** [FILL IN: keep, narrow or remove this category, your choice]
6. **Anyone who has not agreed to be quoted.** Material that needs a person's permission is usable
   only with their explicit yes, each time it is used.
7. **Calls where you train your own team or staff.** A person reads these first. They are never
   mined blind.
8. **Figures you state as fact but cannot back up.** A number you said on a call is not verified
   because you said it. Mark it unverified, or leave it out.
9. **Quotes that flip meaning when lifted alone.** If the line before or after changes what it
   means, leave it out or quote enough to keep the meaning.
10. **Positions you have since dropped.** If you have said you no longer believe it, it is not an
    opinion any more.

## Warnings, not exclusions

- **Wrongly labelled speakers.** Recorders sometimes file the other person's words under your name.
  Check who is speaking either side of a turn before using it. The month files contain only your
  lines unless `include_other_speaker_context` is true in `config.json`, so to check, open the
  source transcript at the line number given.
